Constraint
Constraints define the structure of possible action. In learning systems, they include tasks, assessment conditions, time pressure, and prior knowledge. In media systems, they include information environments and credibility cues. In cycling systems, they include terrain, resistance, drivetrain configuration, and environmental conditions.
Learning Self-regulated learning and metacognition. Language Media interpretation and information response. Machine Cycling telemetry and human–machine regulation.
